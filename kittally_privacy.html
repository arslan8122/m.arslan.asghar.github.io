Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 September 2026

At a glance

KitTally stores your equipment records on your device. You choose when to export or share them. The current Android release configuration has advertising disabled and in-app purchases unconfigured. No KitTally account is required.

1. Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Arslan ("we", "us") handles information in KitTally: Equipment Tracker ("KitTally") and when you contact us. It covers the Android app configuration described here as of the date above.

2. Information you enter in KitTally

The app processes the information you choose to record, including:

  • Kit names, categories, equipment names, quantities and serial numbers.
  • Recipient or borrower details you type, handover dates, due dates, notes and outgoing condition.
  • Return counts, missing or damaged items, corrections and later recovery or repair records.
  • Equipment and condition photos you take or select, and any business name or logo you add.
  • App preferences, drafts and saved progress.

This information is used to organize your equipment, track availability, record handovers and returns, generate reports and restore backups. It is stored in the app's private device storage. KitTally does not upload your equipment database or photos to a server operated by Arslan, and does not offer automatic cloud synchronization.

Only record personal information about someone else when you are authorized to do so. Avoid putting unnecessary sensitive information in notes or photos.

3. Permissions and device features

  • Camera and photos: used when you choose to photograph equipment or select an image. You can use text records without granting these permissions.
  • Files: the system file picker lets you select a backup for import; export and sharing use destinations you choose.
  • Notifications: if you enable reminders and grant permission, your device can display local due-date reminders. Notification details may be visible on your lock screen according to your device settings.

The app does not request access to your contact list, precise location or microphone for its equipment-recording features. You can manage permissions in Android Settings.

4. Exports, sharing and operating-system backups

When you export a PDF or backup, the file may contain equipment details, recipient information, notes, serial numbers and, where included by the export, photos. KitTally sends the file to another app or destination only through the export or sharing action you initiate. The recipient, storage service or sharing app then handles that copy under its own policies.

KitTally backup files are not encrypted by KitTally. Store and share them carefully. We cannot remove copies from another person's device or a service you selected.

Android's own backup, device-transfer or restore features may copy app data according to your device, account and manufacturer settings. The current app permits operating-system backup. This is separate from KitTally's manual export feature. Review your Android backup settings and the relevant provider's policy.

5. Advertising, purchases and analytics

Advertising is disabled in the current Android release configuration. Although the app includes Google Mobile Ads and its consent library, KitTally does not initialize its advertising integration, request ads or fetch remote ad settings while the master advertising switch is disabled. Equipment records and photos are not supplied to advertisers.

Purchases are not configured in this release configuration. The RevenueCat purchase library is included, but KitTally does not initialize it without a configured billing key. No purchase can be completed through that unconfigured integration.

KitTally does not include a separate usage-analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Google Play and Android may independently process installation, security and diagnostic information under their own settings and policies. See Google's Privacy Policy.

If a future version enables advertising, purchase processing or another online service, we will update this policy to describe the actual information processed, providers, purposes and choices, and obtain consent where required before the relevant processing begins. This notice is not consent to future advertising.

6. Support messages and this website

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you send. We use these to respond and investigate the issue. Do not send a full backup unless it is needed and you intend to share its contents. Our email provider processes the message as part of providing email service.

These policy pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising or tracking cookies added by Arslan. The website hosting provider may process normal request information, such as IP address, browser information and access time, to deliver and secure the pages. If hosted on GitHub Pages, see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

7. Retention and deletion

Local records remain on your device until you remove them or erase the app's data. Archiving a kit does not delete its records, and historical records or recovery copies can retain previously entered information.

To erase the app's local records, open Settings → Delete local data → Delete all local data and confirm. This removes kits, handovers, photos stored by KitTally, drafts and recovery data, and cancels app reminders. You can also clear app storage through Android Settings. Deleting a KitTally copy does not delete the original photo from your photo library.

Exported PDFs, manual backups, shared files and operating-system backups must be deleted separately from their storage locations. A later restore can bring backed-up records back. We cannot remotely access or delete your local equipment database.

Support correspondence is retained only as long as reasonably needed to handle your request, follow-up issues, legal obligations or disputes. You may ask us to delete it; where retention is legally necessary, we will explain that when applicable. There is no KitTally account to delete. If purchases become available, deleting app data will not cancel a store subscription.

8. Security and your choices

We rely on the operating system's app storage protections and take reasonable care with information you send us. No device or transmission is completely secure. Protect your device with a lock, maintain backups and restrict access to exported files.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, object to certain processing, or complain to your local data protection authority. You can manage local records directly in the app and contact us about information held in support correspondence. We may need to verify a request before acting on it.

Core on-device processing supports the functionality you request. Where applicable law requires a legal basis for information we receive, support processing is based on responding to your request and our legitimate interest in resolving issues; legal retention is based on applicable obligations. Optional permissions remain under your control.

9. Children

KitTally is a general equipment-management tool and is not designed specifically for children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13 through support. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us, contact us so we can address it. Local records remain controlled by the person using the device.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated in the app or store listing where appropriate, and additional consent will be requested where required.
