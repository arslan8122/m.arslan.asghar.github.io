Luma – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Your photos stay on your device. Luma organizes your library locally, and you decide what to remove.

1. About Luma

Luma: Photo Cleanup (Android package app.luma.photocleanup) is provided by M Arslan Asghar. It helps you review photos and videos and remove only files you choose. You do not need an account.

2. Photos, videos and device permissions

Luma requests photo and video library access before scanning. With your permission, it reads media and metadata such as filenames, capture dates, dimensions, duration and file sizes to organize your library, display previews and help you review files. It also reads available device storage.

This processing takes place on your device. Luma does not upload your photos, videos, thumbnails or media metadata to the developer or to an advertising or purchase provider. Luma does not request access to your contacts, microphone or precise location.

You can deny or revoke permission in Android settings. Without permission, library scanning and cleanup cannot work. Limited access includes only media you share; full library access is required for Luma’s deletion verification.

3. Deletion and locally stored information

You choose files and review your selection before deletion. Android may ask you to confirm removal. Deleted media may remain in your device’s Trash or Recently Deleted folder and continue using storage until that folder is emptied according to your device’s rules.

Luma stores cleanup history locally, including dates, numbers of files removed and their known total size. It also stores an ad-frequency counter locally. The media index used for review is held in app memory. Luma does not maintain an online copy of your library or cleanup history.

Local app data remains until you clear app storage or uninstall Luma, subject to your device’s backup and restore settings. Clearing app data does not delete your remaining photos or videos. We cannot restore media you have deleted.

4. Advertising and purchases in this release

Advertising is disabled in the initial Android release (version 0.1.0). Luma does not request or display ads in this release. Purchase services are not configured in this release, and paid plans are unavailable.

The application includes Google Mobile Ads and RevenueCat integrations for possible future releases. The current configuration does not initialize these services for advertising or purchases. Before enabling services that change data handling, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety information and request consent where required.

For information about these providers, see Google’s Privacy Policy and RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy. Google Play and Android separately process information under their own policies when you download or use their services.

5. Collection, sharing and support

Luma does not operate an account system or a server receiving your media, and the current release does not use an app analytics service. We do not sell your personal information or share your media with advertisers.

If you email us, we receive your email address and any information or attachments you choose to provide. We use that information to respond and resolve the request. Please avoid sending private photos or videos unless necessary for your request. Support messages are kept only as needed to handle the issue, maintain relevant records or meet legal obligations; you may contact us to request deletion.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

You can change photo permissions or clear local app data in Android settings. You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information you provided through support. Rights available to you depend on applicable law. We do not have remote access to your device library, so changes to that library must be made on your device.

7. Security

On-device media processing limits the need to transmit your private files. Your device’s lock, permissions and backup settings help protect locally stored data. No storage or communication method is completely secure. Please use device security controls and keep backups of media you want to retain.

8. Children’s privacy

Luma is a general photo-management utility and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect children’s personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support, contact us so we can address it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when app functionality or data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be communicated through the app or its store listing where appropriate.
